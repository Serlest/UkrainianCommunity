# UAC News scope coordination (active)

The active v3 operation uses separate search state for the nine federal states and Austria-wide news without importing the old v2 candidate backlog.

## State layout

- `source-registry.json`: one entry per canonical source URL with its assigned scopes. A source shared by several scopes is fetched once.
- `shared-source-state.json`: fetch observations for those unique sources.
- `scopes/<scope>.json`: separate source cursors and final candidate decisions for each of the ten scopes. All cursors begin as unverified (`null`). No pending editorial candidates are imported or stored here.
- `snapshots/<runId>.json`: immutable results of one shared source scan. `newsScopeBatch.mjs` selects the observations assigned to one scope without making another network request.

`prepareNewsScopes.mjs` previews the layout by default and writes it only with `--write`. It never edits the v2 file. Two historical RIS query URLs with no assigned scope are retained in the registry as inactive review items.

`newsSharedScan.mjs` also previews by default. `--run` performs a real network scan and creates a snapshot for the active coordinator automation. A second scan with the same run ID is refused. Its result is source discovery, not a publication decision or proof of complete source coverage. Article relevance, date, official evidence, and territory still require editorial checks. Salzburg Verkehr's traffic listing is loaded from its official public JSON feed because the HTML page renders article links with JavaScript. Wiener Linien news uses its official `tramwm.wienerlinien.at/web/guest/news` listing because `www.wienerlinien.at/news` can return a bot-protection page with HTTP 200 and no articles; if either official listing is blocked, mark that source partial rather than a successful zero.

The existing `contentPublishingLocalBridge.mjs` remains the only publisher. It uses deterministic IDs and Firestore identity locks with atomic create-only writes. Event research keeps `content-work.lock`; this editorial-work lock does not block news. Every live news or event write acquires the shared short-lived `content-publish.lock`, which serializes final inventory/reservation/write/read-back transactions across both collections. News also holds `progress-v2/news-austria-regions.json.lock` to serialize news passes. The publisher's cross-kind identity lock and atomic create-only commit remain an additional duplicate guard. Verify the separate production read-back before recording success.

## One-pass candidate decisions

`newsScopeDecisions.mjs <root> <candidate-batch.json>` previews a scope's decision batch. Once the new system is activated, `--apply` atomically records its technical trace. The input lists every article candidate taken from the current discovery pass and one decision per canonical URL. Only `published` and `rejected` are terminal. A rejected candidate needs a concrete reason. A published candidate needs a matching verified production read-back receipt; the recorder checks its ID, approved status, territory, and `allVerified` result. Missing, duplicate, or pending decisions fail the batch; no editorial queue or draft is created.

Discovery must distinguish article candidates from menus, images, and other non-content links before handing the batch to the recorder. A partial source fetch or uncertain publication is a **partial/failed run**, not a successful empty pass. Keep the affected source cursor unchanged and reconcile any uncertain production write before the next attempt. The scope state contains only final URL decisions and a small run summary; full article text and images are not copied into it. Old v2 pending records remain historical and are never imported into v3. The active nationwide chat has separately been instructed not to process that old backlog.

Major transport disruptions affecting connections across several federal states belong in the `austria` news scope. Publish one nationwide article rather than separate copies for each state; the app includes Austria-wide news in every state's feed. A regional publisher identity used for the write does not change the article's `regionScope` or `federalState`.

The ten territorial chats and coordinator scan run on the schedule below. `manifest.json` must report `active`; if it does not, stop the pass and investigate rather than falling back to v2.

## Editorial gate for a fresh news article

User-authorized source rule, 16 October 2026: **one directly opened official authority/operator publication or one approved professional editorial article is sufficient**. Do not search for or require a second independent source, publisher-group separation or a separate official press release. Link to the actual page where the facts were read. Additional sources are optional; do not fabricate them or label same-origin material independent. A feed headline, inaccessible teaser or empty page is insufficient because the facts themselves have not been read. Today-date, territory, factual UK/DE, illustration, dry run, locks and production read-back still apply.

ASFINAG live transport/safety notices can use the checked official live-map/list URL, without a separate permanent incident link. Set manifest `sourcePolicy: "asfinag_official_notice"`; the official-mode review includes `activeNoticeConfirmed: true`, concrete `noticeEvidence`, a reader action and a source check within ten minutes. `primaryPageKind: "live_notice_list"` is accepted. These extra fields establish the actual current entry; they are not a requirement for another source.

Use `newsPublishingLocalBridge.mjs publish <manifest.json> --review <review.json> --receipt <editorial-receipt.json> <publisher options>` for every v3 news publication, including the dry run. It validates the editorial package immediately before invoking the existing publisher. The gate requires a publication or material-update **date of today in Europe/Vienna** on the opened direct primary article; a primary site from the approved scope registry; a directly opened article or notice; and a concrete practical effect and affected audience. Record an evidenced reader action when available, or `readerRelevance` explaining the material service/cost/access change when no immediate action is needed. Do not reject an important future service change solely because applications have not opened; never invent an application route. An official date without hours and minutes is sufficient when it is today. Do not invent a source time. The manifest's `publishedAt` is the time UAC publishes the article, set immediately before the dry run/live attempt and no more than 10 minutes before each gate check. Refresh the manifest and repeat the dry run if preparation exceeds that window.

The review JSON records `scope`, `sourceEventDate` (`YYYY-MM-DD` as shown on the direct primary article or material update in the chosen evidence route), `sourceEventKind`, `primaryURL`, `primaryCheckedAt`, `primaryOpened`, `primaryPageKind`, `officialAuthorityConfirmed` (official mode), `practicalEffect`, `territoryEvidence`, `affectedPeople`, either `readerAction` or substantive `readerRelevance`, `ukDeFactParityConfirmed`, `originalTextConfirmed`, and `excludedTopicsAbsent`. If an exact official timestamp exists, record it as optional `sourceEventAt`; it must agree with `sourceEventDate` in Europe/Vienna. A material update additionally needs `materialChange` and a direct primary update date of today. Never substitute a listing timestamp or guess a source time. Its `image` records `tool: "image_gen"`, `generatedForURL`, `generatedAt`, `prompt`, `sha256`, `photorealisticReviewed`, `topicMatchReviewed`, and `sourceImageCopied: false`. The manifest must use an absolute `imagePath` so the reviewed file is the file sent to the publisher. The gate hashes the actual manifest and cover, checks substantial Ukrainian and German bodies, and writes a bound technical receipt. Image originality, source interpretation, and factual parity still require an actual editorial review; boolean fields alone cannot prove them.

The app's news detail screen displays the direct `sourceURL` as a clickable source section in both language views. The existing publisher writes full `localizations.uk` and `localizations.de` bodies and accepts one approved primary source; optional secondary sources must be correctly described. The v3 candidate decision recorder requires the editorial receipt and a matching production read-back before it can record `published`.

### Two evidence routes

`evidenceMode: "official"` uses one approved official structure/operator as `verificationRole: "officialPrimary"`, with `officialAuthorityConfirmed: true`. No second source is required.

`evidenceMode: "editorial"` uses one approved professional newspaper or ORF direct article as `editorialPrimary`. Set `storyClass: "public_interest"`, `primaryPublisherConfirmed: true`, `primaryFullTextVerified: true` and `paywallRestricted: false` after actual checks. No independent-source, independent-origin or separate official-primary requirement applies. Report what the opened article establishes, attribute assertions to that source, and do not invent legal/medical/financial advice. The opened article must be dated today. A paid teaser is not full evidence; do not bypass access controls. MeinBezirk community contributions remain discovery leads, not automatically professional editorial evidence. Keep substantial regional economy, housing, infrastructure, education, services and community reporting, with concrete reader relevance even without immediate action.

### Relevance and importance

Select by concrete public interest: effects on people, households, jobs, costs, housing, education, health, infrastructure, rights or community life; scale and magnitude; and what is new today. Urgent active safety/service restrictions come first, then substantial economic/social developments and useful local changes. Small communities can have important news; size is not a quota or minimum threshold. One open approved primary remains sufficient.

A new representative study, employment outlook or material economic finding can qualify with `readerRelevance`. Do not require a newly adopted law, changed service, immediate application, open enrollment or official press release as a universal condition. The 17 October report on 56,600 business successions/almost 780,000 jobs exemplifies substantial national economic relevance; the housing survey and employment report exemplify regional relevance. State sponsor/method/limits where available; distinguish findings from proposals and forecasts from guarantees. Routine publicity, unsupported opinion or generic features can still be rejected. Explain actual lack of significance instead of substituting “no adopted measure” for an assessment.

## Discovery evidence and reporting

### Correcting or updating an existing published article

The ordinary publisher remains create-only. For a verified material update or a concrete correction of an existing approved UAC news record, use `node functions/scripts/newsAmendPublished.mjs <config.json>` for a read-only dry run, then the same command with `--apply` under both owned news locks. This updates the same deterministic ID; never change the URL or create a duplicate to evade an identity conflict. A newly announced replacement bus or the end of a previously published restriction can require an in-place update. An ended incident is not a reason to create a new retrospective article.

Keep a separate amendment artifact directory. Config fields: `baselineManifestPath` (the manifest matching current production), `baselineReviewPath` (the original cover provenance), `manifestPath` (corrected complete UK/DE package), `reviewPath`, `organizationId`, `planPath`, `resultPath`, optionally `readbackPath`, and the live lock's `runId` and `threadId`. The review must use `sourceEventKind: "material_update"`, explain `materialChange`, pass the existing news editorial gate and recheck the primary within ten minutes. The same approved source suffices. Set `publishedAt` to the actual UAC amendment time. Keep the source, territory, publisher, category and existing reviewed thematic cover unchanged. A retained cover may be older than the normal 24-hour freshness limit: require exact equality with the baseline cover provenance, verify local source bytes against their original SHA-256, reproduce the publisher's canonical JPEG conversion, and freshly read production to confirm the served cover hash matches that conversion. No new or changed cover is permitted for a text-only amendment.

The dry run records the full production baseline and Firestore `updateTime`. Live apply checks that baseline, both owned unexpired news locks (publication and progress-state) and the exact dry-run hashes, then patches only bilingual text/summary and UAC timestamps with an atomic `updateTime` precondition. Counters, image, ownership, territory and identity locks are preserved. A conflict or uncertain response requires production reconciliation before another attempt. Do not blindly retry a PATCH.

After the write the command verifies production and stores `<root>/amendments/<contentId>.json`, pointing to the latest verified manifest and receipt. This is an audit index of an already published article, not a candidate queue. Subsequent checks must use that manifest; the original creation manifest/receipt describe the historical version. Keep closed reports and decisions intact; record changes in a separate amendment report. Do not amend an article while another chat is actively editing that same event. Release both news locks in `finally`.

Snapshots record `sourcePolicyVersion`. Policy activation archives registries in `source-policies/v<version>.json`; `newsScopeBatch.mjs` automatically uses the snapshot's version. Compare an existing snapshot with that archived registry's expected count, rather than today's manifest count. The transition wave `news-2026-10-16T09` retains its original 25-source v1 snapshot; v2 snapshots retain their 16-source registry. Newly created snapshots after v4 activation use the 55-source v4 registry; retained v3 snapshots keep their 53-source registry. Never rewrite old snapshots or closed reports to match a later policy.

The core RSS parser supports RSS 2.0 and ORF national RDF, retains headline, short description, exact feed timestamp and Europe/Vienna calendar date, and fails visibly on a changed/empty feed. Feed dates do not prove the opened primary article date. Official AMS, AGES, BMF and ÖIF extractors retain article links rather than menus and archive indexes. `--today` merges observations without turning an old observation into a fresh check and without masking current source failures.

Use `node functions/scripts/newsReadArticle.mjs <direct-article-URL>` when reading a primary article. For Land Kärnten `/Service/News?nid=...`, this reads both the public article URL and its official `/Service/News/dyncontent?nid=...` body loader. Keep the public URL as `sourceURL`. A shell alone is not a verified article; dates found in the body are candidates and their publication/update labels still require review.

Territorial reports must state the actual discovery basis, current-day leads reviewed, direct primary articles opened, evidence modes, publication IDs, rejection reasons and unresolved verification/coverage gaps. A feed response or a closed candidate batch does not prove that all Austria-wide news was examined. A useful lead with missing primary evidence is `unverified`, not `no_practical_effect`; a loader failure makes coverage partial. In particular, a headline that signals an ongoing scam, fraud trend, safety instruction, service restriction, cost, job, housing or other concrete reader impact must not be rejected as `no_practical_effect` by headline triage alone. Open the direct article and assess its actual public-interest value; if the lead is from MeinBezirk or another unapproved source and no approved primary has been read, record it as `unverified` with the missing evidence, not as a verified zero-impact story. A headline that clearly describes a routine award or event may be triaged as low relevance only when it does not also signal an ongoing practical issue. Existing reports and snapshots remain immutable. No news quota, drafts or candidate queue is introduced.
